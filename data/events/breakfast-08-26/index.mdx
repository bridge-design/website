Join us for another **Casual Breakfast** (on design systems and not only) — our morning meetup for designers and developers to share coffee, ideas, and insights.

This session features two speakers:

[**Stefan Hoth**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stefanhoth/), an Engineering Leader, will talk about **"How I designed, built and shipped StarLog"** — a local-first app that turns rough memories into interview-ready STAR stories, built using Claude and a team of agents. Stefan will share where putting AI inside the product actually earned its place.

[**Oleg Klimakov**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/klimakovoleg/), a Lead Frontend Developer, will present **"The Year I Started Shipping ⛵"**. A year ago he thought shipping side projects was for other people — now he's doing it ten times a year.

Our breakfasts are kept intentionally low-key and open to conversation. Whether you're a designer, engineer, or somewhere in between, you're welcome to pull up a chair, bring your coffee, and jump in.
