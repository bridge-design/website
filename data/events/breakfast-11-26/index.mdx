On November 4th, 2026, we hosted another **Casual Breakfast** (on design systems, AI and not only) — coffee, ideas, and a proper chat.

This was our first American breakfast — yay! Morning coffee on the US west coast, and a later
meeting for folks in Europe: afternoon and early evening.

Our topic was **"Stop paying AI to guess: cutting the correction loop in design-to-code"**.
A lot of AI's cost in UI work comes from generating, spotting wrong values, and re-prompting.
Giving the model the exact design values up front cuts that loop.

Speaker:

- [**Amrutha Kollu**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/amrutha-kollu/) — Full-Stack Engineer, AI Products & Design Systems

Our breakfasts are kept intentionally low-key and open to conversation. Whether you're a designer, engineer, or somewhere in between, you're welcome to pull up a chair, bring your coffee, and jump in.
