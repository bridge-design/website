Join us for another **Casual Breakfast** (on design systems, AI and not only): coffee, ideas,
and a proper chat.

This is our first American breakfast — yay! Morning coffee on the US west coast, and a later
meeting for folks in Europe: afternoon and early evening.

Our topic is **"Stop paying AI to guess: cutting the correction loop in design-to-code"**.
A lot of AI's cost in UI work comes from generating, spotting wrong values, and re-prompting.
Giving the model the exact design values up front cuts that loop.

Speaker:
