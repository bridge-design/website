This time: less presentation and more workshop!

On September 4th, 2026, we hosted another **Casual Breakfast** (on design systems, AI and not only) — coffee, ideas, and a proper chat.

Our topic was **"Design System Teams and Governance"**. Who designs and builds a design system?
Who runs it day after day? Who works on adoption? The answers can look totally different
depending on company size and structure, short- and mid-term goals, or the resources you have.
We walked through different options together, and everyone was welcome to jump in with their
own experience and questions.

Meeting facilitators:

- [**Rafael Sepeda**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rafaelsepeda/), Design Systems Leader | Team Builder & Strategy Owner
- [**Varya Stepanova**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/varyastepanova/), Design Systems consultant, manager & engineer

Our breakfasts are kept intentionally low-key and open to conversation. Whether you're a designer, engineer, or somewhere in between, you're welcome to pull up a chair, bring your coffee, and jump in.
