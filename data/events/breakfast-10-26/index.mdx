On October 21st, 2026, we hosted another **Casual Breakfast** (on design systems, AI and not only) — coffee, ideas, and a proper chat.

Our topic was **"Creating Digital Products"**. Two speakers shared how they approach
creating digital products: from first ideas and early chaos to shipping something real.

Speakers:

- [**Heidi Suutari**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/heidisuutari/) — Vibe coding, AI automations & Digital Marketing
- [**Anja Petrović**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/anjapetrovic/) — Design and vibe coding for the zero-to-one journey

Our breakfasts are kept intentionally low-key and open to conversation. Whether you're a designer, engineer, or somewhere in between, you're welcome to pull up a chair, bring your coffee, and jump in.
