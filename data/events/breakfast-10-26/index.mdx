and a proper chat.

Our topic is **"Creating Digital Products"**. Two speakers will share how they approach
creating digital products: from first ideas and early chaos to shipping something real.

Speakers:

- [**Heidi Suutari**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/heidisuutari/) — Vibe coding, AI automations & Digital Marketing
- [**Anja Petrović**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/anjapetrovic/) — Design and vide coding for the zero-to-one journey

As usual: low-key, open conversation. You can join as a designer, engineer, or somewhere in between.
Bring your coffee and something to bite, or stay fully focused on chat, we don't mind either.
