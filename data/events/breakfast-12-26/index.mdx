Join us for another **Casual Breakfast** (on design systems, AI and not only): coffee, ideas,
and a proper chat.

Our topic is **"JSON in, Figma out"**: how a design systems engineer re-architected our
themes with an AI agent. Design explorations move fast, and design systems teams rarely join
them from day one. Patrycja's themes started as a small experiment with a couple of extra
colours. Then the product was rebuilt around them, and the token structure that worked for a
few colours no longer held up.

As an engineer, she's more at home in code than in Figma; yet most of the fix had to happen
there. She defined the target structure as JSON and connected an AI agent to both the token
repository and Figma. With the code as context, the agent helped her re-architect the tokens
and migrate the actual design files onto the new structure, with designers reviewing each step.
The agent let her work in Figma the way she works in code.
