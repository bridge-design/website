This time it is a special edition: a **Casual AI Breakfast** about how we actually use AI in design and engineering.

The format is jam-style. A few short talks from people sharing their real workflows, then open discussion. Close to the ground, no "orchestrating AI agents in the cloud" energy. Just how we are using these tools day to day, what works, what does not.

Three speakers, with short presentations and showcases so we have enough time for discussion:

- [**Anja Petrović**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/anjapetrovic/) on AI design workflows in a startup context
- [**Sergey Bekharsky**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bekharsky/) on MCP: what it is and how to use it
- [**Mikko Häkkinen**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mikkohakkinen/) on AI memory management

The audience is mixed as always: designers and engineers, mostly the kind of engineers who build UIs. As usual: casual, no recording, bring your coffee and/or food.
